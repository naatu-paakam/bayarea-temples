Bay Area Hindu Temples

Ranked by number of reviews — a Navarathri 2026 visiting guide

Review counts are approximate, compiled from public Google listings in October 2026. Ratings out of 5.

1

Shiva-Vishnu Temple, Livermore

1232 Arrowhead Ave, Livermore, CA (Tri-Valley)
~4,800 reviews4.8 ★Durga Pooja Celebrations Oct 11–20

The largest and most famous Hindu temple in the region — grand South Indian architecture with both Shiva and Vishnu sanctums. The clear anchor for Navarathri visits, with a full 10-day Durga Pooja program including Mata Ki Chowki (Oct 16) and Bathukamma (Oct 18).

2

BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir, Milpitas

1430 California Cir, Milpitas, CA
~1,040 reviews4.8 ★

Stunning traditionally carved stone mandir in the Swaminarayan tradition. Serene, beautifully maintained, and a landmark of the South Bay Hindu community.

3

Shri Krishna Vrundavana, San Jose

43 Sunol St, San Jose, CA
~850 reviews4.8 ★

Traditional Udupi-style Krishna temple with a golden mandapam. Known for devotional poojas, prasadam after noon pooja, and a peaceful, spiritual atmosphere.

4

Fremont Hindu Temple (Vedic Dharma Samaj)

3676 Delaware Dr, Fremont, CA
~694 reviews4.7 ★Devi Navarathri special poojas

One of the oldest Hindu temples in the Bay Area (40+ years). Active festival calendar with Devi Navarathri special poojas and Navratri Garba this season.

5

Shirdi Sai Parivaar, Milpitas

1221 California Cir, Milpitas, CA
~683 reviews4.8 ★

Large, well-maintained Sai Baba temple complex with a big prayer hall, Dwarkamai and meditation hall. Evening aarthi around 6:30 PM; free prasadam on Thursdays.

6

Sri Satya Narayana Swamy Devasthanam, Milpitas

475 Los Coches St, Milpitas, CA
~560 reviews4.8 ★

Dedicated to Lord Satya Narayana, popular for Satyanarayana vratams and family poojas. Highly rated by devotees for its priests and service.

7

Sunnyvale Hindu Temple & Community Center

450 Persian Dr, Sunnyvale, CA
~400+ reviews~4.3 ★

Established 1993, spanning several acres with murtis sourced from India. All major deities under one roof, plus educational programs and cultural events.

8

Mandir Shreemaya Krishnadham, San Jose (Alviso)

175 Nortech Pkwy, San Jose, CA
419 reviews4.7 ★

Krishna temple in the Alviso area with an active devotee community and regular cultural programs.

9

Shiv Durga Temple, Santa Clara

3550 Flora Vista Ave, Santa Clara, CA
~250 reviews4.7 ★Shiv–Durga focus, apt for Navarathri

Dedicated to Shiva and Durga — a natural fit for Navarathri darshan. Serves the Hindu community across the Bay Area with regular religious services.

10

Balaji Matha Temple, San Jose

San Jose, CA
138 reviews4.4 ★

Vibrant space for worship of multiple Hindu deities with special poojas and prasadam.

11

Shirdi Sai Darbar, Sunnyvale

255 San Geronimo Way, Sunnyvale, CA
53 reviews5.0 ★

Small, beloved Sai Baba temple with a perfect 5-star rating from devotees. Open 7 AM–9 PM daily.

12

Nithyanandeshwara Hindu Temple, Milpitas

513 Los Coches St, Milpitas, CA
49 reviews4.8 ★

Shaivite temple in Milpitas with daily worship and a dedicated devotee base.

13

Sri Krishna Balaram Temple (ISKCON), Sunnyvale

1235 Reamwood Ave, Sunnyvale, CA
4.8 ★

ISKCON-affiliated Krishna-Balaram temple run by the India Heritage Foundation. Kirtans, Sunday programs, and festival celebrations.

14

Vedanta Society of Northern California, San Francisco

2323 Vallejo St, San Francisco, CA
Historic

Historic Vedanta center (est. 1900) — more a spiritual and lecture center in the Ramakrishna tradition than a conventional temple, but a landmark of Hinduism in the West.
